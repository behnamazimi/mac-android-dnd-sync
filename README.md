# Focus (Do Not Disturb) Sync

**Keep Focus in lockstep between your Mac and Android phone**

Flip Focus on either device and the other follows, at home or away.

</div>

## Install

<!-- prettier-ignore -->
> [!NOTE]
> Both apps are signed and verified, and waiting for App Store and Play
> Store review. Until they are listed, download and install them
> yourself. On Android, allow installs from this source when asked.

Download the `.dmg` and `.apk` from the
[latest GitHub Release](https://github.com/behnamazimi/mac-android-focus-sync/releases/latest).

Needs macOS 14 or later and Android 15.

## Quickstart

Pair both devices, then flip Focus. That's it!

### Mac

1. Open the `.dmg`, drag **Focus Sync** onto **Applications**, and launch
   it.
2. Click the menu-bar icon (no Dock icon) and finish the wizard: allow
   Shortcuts, add the On and Off shortcuts, then optionally **Open at
   login**.
3. Leave the window open on the QR code.

### Android

1. Install the `.apk` and open **Do Not Disturb Sync**.
2. Scan the Mac's QR code, or paste the pairing code. Joining needs the
   internet, even on the same Wi-Fi.
3. Enable **Do Not Disturb Sync** in the system Do Not Disturb access
   list.

## Privacy

We store a link between your devices. We never store whether Do Not
Disturb is on or off.

## Notes

- **Closing the Mac window does not quit the app.** Focus Sync stays in
  the menu bar so your phone can still update this Mac. To quit,
  right-click the icon and choose Quit, or press ⌘Q. After you quit, the
  phone cannot change Focus on this Mac.
- **Reinstalling either app disconnects them.** Open Focus Sync on the
  Mac and scan the QR code from your phone to connect again.
- **The Mac cannot always turn Do Not Disturb off on the phone.** If you
  turn it on from the phone, then off from the Mac, Android may keep it
  on (another mode or the Quick Settings tile is still holding it). You'll
  get **Couldn't turn Do Not Disturb off** notification.
